🏆 What Wins: The 90-Second Scan

Hiring managers rarely clone repos during initial screening — they scan them. Here is what separates hired engineers from tutorial clones:

📊 Rigorous Eval Suites

Implement golden sets, LLM-as-a-judge rubrics, and automated CI gates. "Eval is the new system design" — prove your output doesn't rely on vibes.

⚡ Observability & Tracing

Integrate Langfuse, Phoenix, or OpenTelemetry. Show trace trees, token counts, and cost-per-call metrics directly in your README.

🛡️ Error Handling & Guardrails

Implement retry loops with exponential backoff, rate limit handlers, circuit breakers, and schema validation with Pydantic.

🚀 Live Deployment URL

Deploy on Vercel, Modal, Railway, or Hugging Face. Reviewers click live links; nobody runs local environment setup from scratch.
